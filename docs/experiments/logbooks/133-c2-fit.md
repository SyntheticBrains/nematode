# 133: Phase 10 — C.2's Registered Fit Fails on tph-1 Alone: Its Wild Type Passes Every Population, and Its Serotonin Mutant Stays Inert, as in Its Failed Recovery Control

**Status**: `completed` — **C.2 fails its registered verdict, on tph-1 alone.** The encoded connectome, fitted once from the box's centre by the registered staged procedure, passes the wild type in 5 of 5 registered populations (15 of 15 with 10 more), and tax-4 and pdfr-1 in every one. tph-1 passes in none: removing serotonin changes nothing in the fitted worm.

- **The maintainer's decision before the run:** the fail is read against the failed recovery control, F.0b-ii, which failed the same way (gain 0.635, tph-1 inert). It is not attributed to the model.
- **Unresolved:** why the procedure's 2b stage finds tph-1's response in some runs and not in others. Under the final rules it found it in 1 of 3 runs. Part C's diagnosis, that ser-5's strength at 2a's end sets it, does not survive this run.
- **What it conditions:**
  - [Logbook 131](131-c2-clean-start.md)'s 15 of 15 was one route adapted after each reading. Fixed in advance and run fresh, the procedure does not reliably reproduce its tph-1.
  - The neural reading on this answer conditions [Logbook 134](134-fit-first-neural-readings.md)'s "no detectable gain from behaviour fitting". On this answer, as description, the paired class interval lies just above 0.
- **What follows:**
  - Phase 10 closes without W, by the maintainer's decision of 2026-11-23, before the run.
  - C.3 is not run, and C.2 enters C.4 as a failing in-sample row. The maintainer decided both on 2026-11-25, after the read. They are new decisions, not branches of the registration.

## Objective

C.2 is Phase 10's connectome controller: the 302-neuron wiring fixed from data, with the data-derived neuromodulator layer, made a function of 137 named parameters by the compact encoder (Logbooks 122 and 130). Logbook 131 found, in probes adapted after each reading, a route from the box's centre to an answer passing all 24 wild-type statistics and the three mutants in 15 of 15 populations, once on-food reversals follow the worm's own speed (Logbook 132). Its stated limit was that the route had been adapted along the way: "The registered fit has to fix its procedure in advance and run it fresh."

This change fixed that procedure, ran it on discarded seeds until a readiness pilot passed, checked it by recovering a known answer (F.0b-ii), and then ran it once on registered seeds. The question: **does C.2's encoded connectome, fitted by a procedure fixed in advance, pass the wild type and its encodable mutants?**

## Method

The launch record holds the full design. In brief:

- **The controller and arm:** the encoded connectome under the procedure's options. These are per-ligand tying (137 parameters), the soft-capped readout with pool centres, the graded box with the inhibition floor at 0.09, PDFR-1's and SER-5's cited signs, and slow speed noise. It runs on `benchmark_grade.ARMS["connectome_c2_following"]`, the encoded arm with `continuous.on_food_reversal_speed: follows_run_speed`. Every stage runs at 200 animals a candidate and population 48, except stage 1.

- **The procedure** (sep-CMA, bounds [0, 1], fixed budgets):

  | Stage | Fits | Objective | Start | Generations |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | 77 sense-to-output parameters | D_fit's four families | the box centre, σ₀ 0.2, 64 animals, population 24 | 150 |
  | 2a | all 137 | the same | stage 1's answer, σ₀ 0.1 | 300 |
  | 2b | all 137 | all six families, the mutants at half, chemotaxis presence | a new isotropic search at 2a's answer, σ₀ 0.05 | 200 |
  | noise | — | 2b's answer on 30 populations, each statistic's SD | | |
  | final | all 137 | 2b's, plus each statistic's chance of failing and a hinge 0.3 inside each bar | 2b's search state | 50 |

  Every stage carries the lawn, saturation and responsiveness guards. The first half (stage 1 and 2a) is started up to six times. A start is dropped at the first of 2a's three readings it fails:

  - the lawn: time on the lawn at least 0.95 over generations 40–60;
  - the descent: D_fit at most 4.25 over 130–150;
  - the bar: D_fit at most 2.5 over 280–300.

- **The verdict:** C.2 passes when, on the first 5 of 15 graded populations of 200 (graded with presence), the wild type passes in at least 3, and tph-1 and tax-4 each in at least 3. The mutants are graded on the encoding table's statistics: tph-1 on its roaming fraction, direction more. It is in-sample: every graded statistic and both resolved mutants are fitted. pdfr-1 is graded and reported.

- **Reported beside, never re-read as the verdict:**

  - the 10 further populations;
  - the held-out reading;
  - every readout and the plateau reading;
  - the reversal share against Roberts' 17.3%;
  - the cost per stage;
  - the restart spread against the pilot;
  - the neural reading.

- **Seeds** (registered, unused before the run): base 1,280,000,000; CMA seeds 5811–5814, with restarts from 5841; the grade's animals 1,298,000,000 + 200·g + i for g < 15.

### Why tph-1 fails: 2b never found the response

The response counts as found at the first 2b generation where at least 10% of candidates show a tph-1 roaming difference of 0.05 or more. This is Part C's rule.

- **2b, generations 1–200:** of the 9,598 candidates with a defined tph-1 difference (of 9,600; generations 13 and 14 each have one without), one reached 0.05: a candidate at generation 13 (+0.51) whose worm left the lawn (time on the lawn 0.003, D_fit 6.77). No generation came near the rule.
- **The final stage:** of 2,400 candidates, the largest difference is 0.012 (`probes/fit_serotonin.json`).

Part C's diagnosis read the four earlier runs that reached 2b as suggesting that how strong 2a left the ser-5 couple sets whether 2b finds the response. With this run added (`probes/serotonin_history_complete.json` and `probes/fit_serotonin.json`):

| Run | ser-5 at 2a's end | ser-5 at 2b's end | The other serotonin couple at 2b's end | Response found in 2b |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sixth pilot | 2.31 | 2.86 | 0.003 | generation 2 |
| fifth pilot | 0.19 | 1.08 | 0.33 | generation 177 |
| fourth pilot | 0.052 | 0.20 | 0.65 | generation 81 |
| **the registered fit** | **0.076** | **1.28** | **0.001** | **never** |
| F.0b-ii's recovery | 0.0039 | 0.016 | 0.003 | never |

**ser-5's strength does not order the outcome.**

- **At 2a's end,** this fit's ser-5 sits between the fourth pilot's and the fifth's, both of which found the response.
- **At 2b's end,** this fit's 2b had raised ser-5 to 1.28, above the fifth pilot's 1.08 at the same point. It still found nothing.
- **That agrees with two earlier readings:**
  - In Logbook 131, raising ser-5 to 1.44 alone left tph-1 inert.
  - In Part C's swap check, the recovery's answer with the truth's ser-5 gave +0.048, against the truth's +0.27.
- **A hypothesis, not tested here:** in the sixth pilot's answer ser-5 is necessary (Part C's swap check), but it is not sufficient in the recovery's answer or in this fit. Whether the other runs needed it is not known, and neither is what else they needed. 2b's search found the response in three of five runs and missed it in two.
- **No single value separates the runs.** Every run that found the response ended 2b with ser-5 above 2, or the other serotonin couple above 0.3. That observation is made after the fact, on five runs, and it is not tested here.

## What this establishes, and what it does not

**Establishes.**

- **Fitted once by the registered procedure, from the box's centre on registered seeds, C.2 fails its verdict on tph-1 alone.**
- **Its wild type passes in every registered and further population, in-sample.** Its tax-4 and pdfr-1 pass in every one.
- **Its fail matches its failed recovery control.** F.0b-ii, run by the same procedure toward a truth whose tph-1 responds, also ended with tph-1 inert. Under the final rules the procedure produced tph-1's response in 1 of 3 runs, and over the five runs that reached 2b in 3.
- **Under the final rules the wild type passed in two of the three runs and failed in one:** this fit's 5 of 5 and the sixth pilot's 3 of 5, against the recovery's 0 of 5 against the bands. Three runs give no rate for either.

**Leaves unresolved.**

- **What decides whether 2b finds tph-1's response.** ser-5's strength alone does not. The other serotonin couple, the monoamine timescale and the network downstream are all candidates, untested.
- **Whether a longer 2b, or the mutants weighted differently, would find it.** No repair runs in Phase 10 (the cap, point 2).
- **The under-reversing,** 5.6% of the time against 17.3%. The benchmark reports it and does not grade it, so this fit was free to give it up.

**Does not establish.**

- **That C.2's model cannot represent tph-1.** The sixth pilot's answer, under the same rules, passes tph-1 in 15 of 15.
- **That the wild-type pass generalises.** It is in-sample, and the held-out reading is poor on both held-out sets.
- **A rate of failure for the procedure.** Three runs under its final rules cannot give one.
- **A claim of neural fidelity.** The neural reading's gain over the unfitted network is one answer's, described, at the edge of its interval.

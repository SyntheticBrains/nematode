## Objective

On benchmark v4, C.1 (v3)'s registered answer fails the wild type in every population, on the on-lawn onset rate ([Logbook 124](124-benchmark-v4.md)). [Logbook 125](125-c1-v4-diagnosis.md) traced that to the model:

- One dwelling reversal rate sets reversals on food and off it.
- With its own on-food rate (`r_dwell_on_food`), a fit continued from the v3 answer passed 4 of 5 discarded populations.
- No run from the box centre reached that basin.

The refit was deferred until C.4 approached. The maintainer's direction decision of 2026-11-23 then chose a warm start under a dated D38 amendment, with a new pilot.

Under D37 as amended, the refit also fits C.1's two encodable mutants, tph-1 and tax-4, together half the objective, as C.2's fit did. **The question: does C.1's model, refitted from its v3 answer, pass v4's wild type with its encodable mutants?**

## Method

Launch record 136 holds the full design. In brief:

- **The model:** `BatchedMechanistic` with C.1 (v3)'s 25 parameters, plus `r_dwell_on_food` on `r_dwell`'s prior (26 dimensions).
- **The start:** C.1 (v3)'s restart-6 answer, with the new coordinate at its box's centre. sep-CMA at σ₀ 0.1, population 16.
- **The body:** the speed-following arm (`benchmark_grade.ARMS["mlp_following"]`), whose on-food reversals follow the worm's speed ([Logbook 132](132-speed-following-reversals.md)).
- **The mutants:** C.1 (v3)'s registered encodings.
  - tph-1 zeroes the switch's dependence on intake.
  - tax-4 zeroes the weathervane gains on and off food, and the dC/dt reversal and omega gains. The edge response to falling food contact is kept, since food contact is the lawn density under the worm, not a gradient.
- **The objective:** the definedness-first ranking score against v4's band centres at n = 200. Each mutant's RMS of compressed, capped z over the encoding table's graded statistics enters at half, by C.2's formula.
- **The stop:** the plateau rule with the σ guard, the definedness guard and a level guard.
- **The pilot:** one restart on discarded seeds (CMA 9467, 696 M), with a provisional cap of 400. Its answer was graded with presence on 5 populations. Nothing further registers unless all of these hold:
  - it stops on the plateau;
  - the wild type, tph-1 and tax-4 each pass in at least 3 of 5;
  - the recovery's preflight checks pass on its own answer;
  - the projected registered compute is at most 48 h.

The code was reviewed twice by independent agents, once as a spec and once as an implementation, and every finding was fixed before Part A.

## Results

### The go rule: fails on the wild type and tax-4

The pilot stopped on its plateau at generation 113 (median score 3.39, σ 0.041), in 1.34 h at 42.7 s a generation. The registered cap would have been 300. The other parts of the go decision held:

- **The preflight check on its own answer** (698.1 M and 698.2 M):
  - On the combined distance, the floor is 0.68 and the start's D 5.10, with the oracle's gain at 1.01.
  - On the wild type's distance, the floor is 0.43 and the start's D 2.15, with the oracle's gain at 0.96.
  - No statistic's definedness flipped.
- **The projected registered compute** is 20.1 h, against the bar of 48 h, and 53.4 h at the cap.

### What the search did

[pilot_reading.json](supporting/136-c1-v4-fit/probes/pilot_reading.json) lists every parameter at the start and at the answer. The large moves:

- **The onset rate:** `r_dwell_on_food` rose from 0.022 to 0.137 per s.
  - At the start, the onset rate fails (z −5.81 on the probe's population).
  - At the answer, it sits at −2.74 to −3.10 across the pilot's 5 populations.
- **tph-1:** the switch's dependence on intake reversed sign, `b_intake` going from +0.39 to −1.09. Removing it now makes the worm roam more, as in real tph-1 worms.
  - tph-1's Δ roaming went from −0.19 at the start (z −9.04 on the probe's population) to +0.26–0.29.
- **The two states' speeds crossed:** `v_roam` fell from 0.060 to 0.022 mm/s, and `v_dwell` rose from 0.012 to 0.032.
  - The roaming windows' speed median fell from 0.037 mm/s at the start (z −0.29) to 0.030, which is z −7 on a band this narrow at n = 200.
  - Meanwhile the wild type's capped D fell slightly, from 1.87 (the start's median of 10, [separation.json](supporting/136-c1-v4-fit/probes/separation.json)) to 1.73–1.82.
  - The ranking score is an RMS, so it lets one statistic go far past its bar while the rest improve. C.2's procedure needed a final hinge for the same reason ([Logbook 131](131-c2-clean-start.md)).
  - Whether the speed was given up for tph-1 or for the onset rate is not tested.
- **Lawn retention moved away from `edge`:** `edge` fell from 1.25 to 0.06, close to its lower bound, while `beta_omega` rose from 1.3 to 3.5 and `beta_reversal` from 3.3 to 4.1.

**tax-4's term was flat throughout.** Its exits difference sat at the z cap of 10 at the start ([separation.json](supporting/136-c1-v4-fit/probes/separation.json): +12.6), and it stayed there.

- A capped term has no slope, so the search had no signal from it.
- At the answer, tax-4's term carries 86–88% of the squared score ([pilot_reading.json](supporting/136-c1-v4-fit/probes/pilot_reading.json)).

### Why tax-4 fails at the probed points: the lawn is held by the steering tax-4 removes

The probe ran after the pilot's no-go and before the decision, as description: [tax4_retention.py.txt](supporting/136-c1-v4-fit/probes/tax4_retention.py.txt), on discarded seeds at 641 M. It graded 16 points with presence:

- the pilot's answer (A) and the start (S), as they are;
- each with `edge`, the lawn response tax-4 keeps, raised up to its box's maximum (5.0);
- each with the dC/dt reversal and omega gains, which tax-4 removes, set to 0;
- each with the weathervane gain also at 0.

(The edge 2.5 rows with betas at 0 are in the record and read the same way.)

- **The betas don't hold the lawn:** zeroing them barely moves the wild type's exits or tax-4's difference.
- **The weathervane gain does.** `kappa_weathervane` sits at −43 at both points, near its box's edge of −50. Without it, the wild type itself exits about 9 times an hour and fails the small lawn.
  - tax-4 then fails on its roaming fraction instead (z 2.80 and 2.60), since the two worms barely differ.
- **`edge` at its maximum** still leaves tax-4 7.6 to 9.6 exits an hour above its wild type. That is more than 20 of the band's scales (0.36 an hour) beyond its centre, against a bar of 2.24.

Real tax-4 worms hold a small lawn almost as well as the wild type: +0.31 exits an hour on the bands' dates. Whatever keeps them there, it is not the tax-4-dependent chemosensation that C.1's model uses for the purpose. At the probed points, none of the model's other lawn-holding mechanisms is strong enough. The probe sets no bound over the rest of the box.

## What this establishes, and what it does not

**It establishes** that at all 16 probed points, C.1's mechanistic model under its registered tax-4 encoding does not hold tax-4 on the small lawn the way real tax-4 worms stay, wherever its wild type holds the lawn.

- At those points, the wild type stays on the lawn by weathervane steering up the food gradient, and tax-4's encoding removes it.
- The one lawn response tax-4 keeps, at its box's maximum, leaves the difference about 20 band scales out at both points probed there.

It also shows that C.1's model can reach tph-1's direction from its v3 answer, and v4's on-lawn onset rate with it, both on discarded seeds, as description.

**It does not establish**:

- **No proof of absence:** this is not a proof over the registered box, and no bound over it is established. The probe tested 16 points around two answers and one mechanism, so the claim covers those points only.
- **No verdict:** it gives no registered verdict on C.1 (v4), since nothing registered ran. The pilot's 0 of 5 is a go rule's reading on discarded seeds.
- **The wild type is not settled:** the wild type's failure on the roaming windows' speed is not shown to be structural. A hinge might have held it, as in C.2's procedure, but this was not tested. tax-4 makes the question moot for the verdict.
- **Real tax-4 worms are not explained:** nothing here says what keeps a real tax-4 worm on a lawn.

**What it conditions.** As description, the pilot's fitted answer mirrors C.2:

- C.2 holds tax-4 (5 of 5) and cannot move tph-1 ([Logbook 133](133-c2-fit.md)).
- The pilot's answer moves tph-1 and fails tax-4.

This is not C.4's contrast, since the two controllers' rows differ in kind. C.2's mutant rows are fitted and in-sample. C.1's are not: it enters through its v3 answer, which was fitted to the wild type alone ([Logbook 121](121-c1-v3-fit.md)), and its v4 refit stopped at the pilot. Its rows are that answer's predictions. On discarded seeds, both fail there: tph-1 and tax-4 each pass 0 of 10 at the start ([separation.json](supporting/136-c1-v4-fit/probes/separation.json)).

- **The recovery (C.1 (v4)'s F.0 control):** *unreachable-with-reason*. Under Part A, nothing registers after a failed go rule.
- **The registered fit and its verdict:** *unreachable-with-reason*. The maintainer closed the refit, and C.1 (v4) has no registered answer.
- **The held-out reading:** *unreachable-with-reason*. It was not run, and the held-out dates are unread.
- **The tax-4 leave-one-out:** *unreachable-with-reason*. It was not run, so there is no prediction from a v4 answer.
- **C.1's row in C.4:** *deferred-with-destination* (C.4).
  - C.1 enters as a failing row, through its v3 answer: it passes v3 in 3 of 5 ([Logbook 121](121-c1-v3-fit.md)) and fails v4 in 0 of 5 ([Logbook 124](124-benchmark-v4.md)), for want of an on-food dwelling reversal rate ([Logbook 125](125-c1-v4-diagnosis.md)).

  - Its tax-4 fails at every point this logbook's probe tested, with no bound over the box. Its tph-1 is reachable from the v3 answer by fitting, as description.

# 121: Phase 10 — C.1, Given Three Missing Mechanisms, Passes the Wild Type on Benchmark v3

**Status**: completed. **C.1 (v3) passes the wild type.** The chosen restart (6, by selection on separate populations) passes 3 of its 5 registered populations at D 1.03–1.14. The two that miss each fail one statistic, the small lawn's roaming-bout length.

Three of the 12 restarts pass the wild type on the registered populations: restart 4 in 5 of 5, restart 0 in 4 of 5, and restart 6 in 3 of 5.

The model is C.1 with three mechanisms it lacked, each found by probes on discarded seeds (25 parameters):

- a per-state speed spread;
- off-food run kinematics;
- an off-food weathervane gain.

## Objective

C.1's 20-parameter model could not reach benchmark v3. C.1b's v2 fits had passed v2's lawns by shuttling back and forth (reversing about half the time off food), and v3 closed that loophole ([Logbook 120](120-benchmark-v3.md)). The maintainer asked first whether the project was ready for another fit, and then for diagnosis before any registration.

## The diagnosis: three missing mechanisms (probes, description)

Every probe was one sep-CMA restart against v3's ranking score with C.1b's guarded stop, on discarded seeds. Each answer was graded with presence on 5 populations:

| Probe | Model | Start | Passing | D | What fails |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| readiness | 20 parameters | box centre | 0 | 3.42–3.56 | dwelling window speed z +7, dwelling bouts, weathervane |
| warm from C.1b | 20 | C.1b's answer | 0 | 2.49–2.70 | the three new statistics (reverses 47%) |
| widened box | 20 | box centre | 0 | 3.09–3.54 | dwelling speeds; the fit runs to the new bounds |
| off-food kinematics | 22 | box centre | 0 | 2.50–2.74 | Chen's onset rate, flips, weathervane |
| speed spread | 24 | box centre | 0 | 1.28–1.46 | the weathervane alone (z +4), in 3 of 5 |
| all three, warm | 25 | the 24's answer | 4 | 0.85–1.04 | one roaming-bout length |
| all three | 25 | box centre (×3) | 0, 3, 0 | 2.5–2.8, 1.08–1.33, 1.44–1.53 | depends on the basin |

1. **One run speed per state.**
   - v2's own reachability worm needed a per-window speed spread to match the graded window-speed medians and 90th percentiles (launch record 115).
   - Without one, C.1b faked the spread by reversing, and C.1 on v3 by extreme tortuosity.
   - With a spread, kinematics pass in all 5 populations, and D falls from about 2.6 to 1.3.
2. **The same kinematics on and off food,** so slow dwelling also slows search and chemotaxis.
3. **One weathervane gain for two cues.**
   - The paired sensitivity tests show the gain is what keeps the worm on the lawn: time on the lawn falls to z −9.5 at half strength.
   - It is not what gives C.1 its chemotaxis: drift and presence barely move at a gain of 0.
   - An off-food gain at a quarter of it fixes Chen's weathervane (z +4.1 → +0.7).

**Ruled out:**

- **the benchmark's assay timing:** Chen's naive worms were "directly removed from food and washed for three times before testing" (Chen et al. 2025), as simulated;
- **the search term:** removing it barely changed anything.

The mechanisms were chosen by probes on all five families. That is why C.1's mutant predictions, not this fit, carry its held-out weight in C.4.

## Method

- **The model:** C.1's 20 parameters on F.0a's box, plus five more on ranges set without search data:

  - `v_off_food` (0.005–0.20 mm/s);
  - `sigma_off_food` (5–60 °/s);
  - `speed_log_sd_dwell` and `speed_log_sd_roam` (0–1.5);
  - `kappa_off_food` (−50 to 50).

  Without them the model is byte-identical: all 55 identity jobs match.

- **The mutant encodings:** tax-4 also removes the off-food gain. The kinematic parameters are kept by every mutant.

- **The recovery:** F.0b-i's rules on the 25-parameter model, at the two passing probe answers, 2 restarts each.

- **The fit:** C.1b's design on v3:

  - 12 box-centre restarts (the maintainer raised it from 8 at approval);
  - the doubly guarded plateau, cap 330;
  - selection on 5 populations per plateaued restart (raised from 3);
  - a verdict at 3 of 5 registered populations with presence.

## What this establishes, and what it does not

- **C.1 can reproduce v3's wild type, given three mechanisms.** Each answers a measured failure, and v2's own reachability work had already needed the first. The pass is at the threshold (3 of 5). Three restarts pass on the registered populations, and the best of them (restart 4) passes all 5.
- **Selection is noisy at this margin.** Restart 4 passes 5 of 5 registered populations but only 2 of 5 selection populations. Restart 6 was chosen on 3 of 5, by the registered rule. That is reported, not re-read.
- **The fit chose its mechanisms by looking.** The added mechanisms were chosen on the benchmark's families, so this wild-type pass is weaker evidence than a blind fit's. C.1's test is its mutant predictions in C.4, under the extended encodings.
- **C.1 under-reverses.** It reverses about 3% of the time, against real worms' 17% off food (Roberts) and 12–16% on lawns (Scheer). v3 cannot see reversal from positions, and its escalation rule covers only over-reversal. **v3 does not constrain reversal from below.**
- **The roaming-bout tension across the two lawns persists:** too long on the small lawn, too short in the uniform well. C.1 had the same weak point on v1 ([Logbook 113](113-c1-fit.md)). Its switching sees lawn density only through intake.

## Corrections and lessons

- **The guarded plateau lets a stuck restart stop.**
  - Restart 5 stopped at generation 56 with a median score near 507, meaning about half its candidates left a statistic undefined. The guard blocks a stop only while a median of 1,000 or more sits in the window.
  - Recovery fit 1 stopped at generation 69 at a score of 5.7.
  - Neither could be chosen, but C.2's registration should require the window's medians to stay below the undefined rank by a margin, or to fall below a level the probes set.
- **Check a model against what the benchmark grades before fitting it.** C.1's model was specified before kinematics were graded, and was never checked against the mechanisms v2's validity work had shown were needed. That cost C.1b's fit and two benchmark revisions to surface. C.2 gets a readiness probe before registration.
- **Seed blocks:** with the 10,000·r restart stride, blocks sit at least 2,000,000 animals apart, and answers move to offset 8,000 under a cap above 312. The tests check it.

## Beside the verdict (description)

1. **One model is stretched between two lawns' roaming bouts.** The chosen fit's roaming bouts run long on the small lawn (z up to 4.68) and short on the uniform well (z down to −2.58). C.1 has one roaming state whose exit depends on intake and satiety, and the real worm's roaming bouts differ between the two lawns more than that dependence lets the fit follow. This is the statistic C.2 and C.4 should watch first.
2. **The registered choice is the restart that only just passes.** Restarts 1 and 5 pass all five populations, with slightly higher S (1.27 and 1.40). The choice by the fit's own objective is the registered rule, and it is not re-read. For C.4, the `unidentified` rule uses all three passing restarts (1, 3 and 5).
3. **Two restarts stopped early on a false plateau.** Restarts 6 and 7 stopped at generations 43 and 40, still at D 5–6. Their median objective improved by less than 0.05 between generations 21–40 and 1–20, so the rule fired at its earliest point. F.0b-i saw none of this, and with 8 restarts it costs nothing here. **C.2's registration should add a guard,** such as a minimum generation or a σ condition, so a stuck restart is not counted as converged.
4. **The restart spread on real data** is a median D of 1.09 (0.66–1.87) between the six converged restarts, and 1.73 over all 28 pairs including the stuck two. That matches F.0b-i's 1.12 on synthetic truths. W's minimum is 1.42, so W needs several fits per arm.
5. **The parameters, read only where F.0b-i recovered them:**
   - **weathervane gain κ_w = −47.3 °/s.** The sign steers toward food: `lateral` is right-minus-left, and a negative turn is a right turn. The magnitude is near the box's limit of 50. The lawn statistics push the fit toward strong steering;
   - **search time τ = 482 s (8.0 min).** López-Cruz's own decay, γ 0.114 per minute, is an 8.8-min time constant. Local search is fitted for C.1 under D37, so this agreement is a fit, not a prediction;
   - **the other 18 parameters** are identified only through their statistics. One of them, the dwelling-state omega hazard, sits at its box's upper limit (0.5 per s). With κ_w near its edge, the box may be constraining the fit, a caution for C.4's comparison rather than a defect of the verdict.

## Conclusions

1. **C.1 passes the wild type**, at the registered threshold: 3 of 5 populations, D 0.95–1.26 against the bands. The primitives and the benchmark can hold a fitted mechanistic worm, so the risk row's pivot does not fire.
2. **Its weak point is roaming-bout length across lawns.** One roaming state cannot take both lawns' bouts.
3. **Restart spread on real data is about 1.1 D,** for W.
4. **The plateau rule needs a guard against stuck restarts** before C.2 registers.

"""Can the final stage restore chemotaxis presence once it is scored? (description; discarded)

The hinge screen (2026-11-19, ``hinge_screen.py``) passed its rule: with Logbook 131's hinge added
to the final stage's objective, the on-lawn onset rate's z rose from -4.31 to a mean of -3.01 over
the readouts at generations 30-50, nothing else failed and every mutant passed. Its answer's
described grade on five fresh populations passed the onset rate in all five but the wild type in
only two: chemotaxis presence failed in three, the log quintile ratio's lower bound below 0. The
objective is blind to presence: the ratio's band is wide, so its value fell from 0.097 to 0.048
with its z moving from -1.69 to -1.76. Presence already failed in 5 of the fifth pilot's 15 grade
populations. Its readouts show the ratio at 0.10-0.34 and the drift at 0.008-0.012 mm/s through 2a,
which does not fit chemotaxis, and both falling in 2b (drift to 0.003). Writing Logbook 131's
sensory gains into the screen's answer never restored presence (``chemotaxis_sweep.py``).

**The screen.** From the hinge screen's search state after its 50 generations (sigma 0.0202), a
new sep-CMA carrying its sigma and per-parameter steps at its answer (``c2_fit.carried``),
population 48 at 200 animals, the registered arm, for 50 generations, on the hinge screen's
objective (the final stage's, all six families, the three mutants at half, the fifth pilot's noise
SDs, the guards, plus the hinge) plus a presence term. Each candidate is graded with presence as
the grade grades it (2,000 resamples of its animals, the lower bound at the 2.5% quantile), and for
each of the two presence statistics k (the drift and the log quintile ratio) with lower bound b_k
and scale s_k, the lower bound's standard deviation over the fifth pilot's 15 grade populations
(0.000183 mm/s and 0.0443), the term adds

    5 x max(0, 2 - b_k / s_k)^2 + 10 x Phi(-b_k / s_k),

the hinge's weight at a target two of its own deviations above 0, and the final stage's failure
weight on its chance of falling below 0; b_k / s_k is floored at -10 where it is undefined or lower.
Readouts at generation 0 and every 10 generations on the fifth pilot's readout animals, with
presence; generation 0 reproduces the hinge screen's generation-50 readout, a check on this setup.

**The rule, fixed before it runs** (2026-11-19). Over the readouts at generations 30, 40 and 50,
the screen **passes** when chemotaxis is present in at least 2 of the 3, the on-lawn onset rate's
mean z is at least -3.0781, every other wild-type statistic is defined at all three and its mean
|z| is at most 3.0781, local search is present in at least 2 of the 3, and each mutant passes in at
least 2 of the 3.

- **Passes:** the final stage can restore presence once it scores it, in the basin the fifth pilot
  chose. The amendment adds the hinge to the final stage and the presence term to 2b and the final
  stage.
- **Fails:** presence does not come back in this basin with everything else held; chemotaxis has
  to enter 2a's objective, where the basin is chosen.

**Reported beside it, never selected on:** every readout's statistics, z, lower bounds and mutant
readings; each generation's median and best score, its best candidate's hinge and presence terms,
and the step size; the last answer graded with presence on 5 fresh populations.

**Seeds (discarded).** Candidates 1,460,000,000 + 200 x (48 x (g - 1) + m) + i (50 generations:
480,000 animals, to 1,460,479,999); readouts on the fifth pilot's 1,376,000,000 + i, i < 200,
reused for reading only; the grade 1,461,000,000 + 200 p + i, p < 5; CMA 9925.

Run from the repository root: ``presence_screen.py [workers]``. Every candidate's record is stored
as it completes, and the strategy after every generation, so a resume continues exactly.
"""

import json
import math
import os
import pickle
import statistics
import sys
import time
from concurrent.futures import ProcessPoolExecutor
from pathlib import Path

import numpy as np
from scipy.stats import norm

sys.path.insert(0, "scripts/analysis")
import benchmark_grade as bg
import c2_fit as fit
import npe_recovery as nr

BASE, CMA_SEED, GRADE_BASE = 1_460_000_000, 9925, 1_461_000_000
POPSIZE, ANIMALS, GENERATIONS, EVERY = 48, 200, 50, 10
READING = (30, 40, 50)
HINGE_WEIGHT, MARGIN = 5.0, 0.3
PRESENCE_TARGET, PRESENCE_FLOOR = 2.0, -10.0
PRESENCE = ("drift_mm_s", "log_quintile_ratio")
ONSET = "activity/onset_rate_per_min"
PILOT = Path("docs/experiments/logbooks/supporting/133-c2-fit/pilot.json")
HINGE_STATE = Path("build/c2-fit/hinge_screen/state.pkl")
HINGE_RECORD = Path("build/c2-fit/hinge_screen.json")
READOUT_SEEDS = fit.RUNS["pilot"].layout.readout_seeds()
OUT = Path("build/c2-fit/presence_screen")
WILD_BAR = float(bg.load_bands_v4()["bars"]["wild_type"])


def compressed(z: float) -> float:
    return fit.v2.COMPRESSION * math.asinh(z / fit.v2.COMPRESSION)


def presence_scales(pilot: dict) -> dict[str, float]:
    """The lower bounds' standard deviation over the fifth pilot's grade populations."""
    return {k: statistics.stdev(g["chemotaxis_presence"]["lower_bounds"][k] for g in pilot["grades"])
            for k in PRESENCE}


class PresenceObjective(fit.Objective):
    """The final stage's objective, the hinge screen's hinge and a term on presence's lower bounds."""

    def __init__(self, *args, presence_sd: dict[str, float], **kwargs):
        super().__init__(*args, **kwargs)
        self.presence_sd = presence_sd

    def hinge(self, record):
        wild_target = self.wild_bar - MARGIN
        excess = 0.0
        for key, centre in self.truth["statistics"].items():
            value = record["statistics"].get(key)
            if value is None or centre is None or not math.isfinite(float(value)):
                continue
            z = (float(value) - float(centre)) / self.scales[key]
            excess += max(0.0, abs(compressed(z)) - wild_target) ** 2
        for m in self.mutants:
            for s in fit.GRADED[m]:
                z = fit.mutant_z(self.truth, record, self.mscales[m][s], m, s)
                excess += max(0.0, abs(compressed(z)) - (self.mutant_bars[m] - MARGIN)) ** 2
        return HINGE_WEIGHT * excess

    def presence(self, record):
        bounds = (record.get("chemotaxis_presence") or {}).get("lower_bounds") or {}
        total = 0.0
        for k in PRESENCE:
            b = bounds.get(k)
            x = b / self.presence_sd[k] if b is not None and math.isfinite(float(b)) else PRESENCE_FLOOR
            x = max(PRESENCE_FLOOR, x)
            total += HINGE_WEIGHT * max(0.0, PRESENCE_TARGET - x) ** 2
            total += fit.FAILURE_WEIGHT * float(norm.cdf(-x))
        return total

    def __call__(self, record):
        return super().__call__(record) + self.hinge(record) + self.presence(record)


def _job(item):
    generation, member, seeds, z = item
    began = time.perf_counter()
    record = fit.simulate_point(z, seeds, assays=None, mutants=fit.MUTANTS, presence=True)
    return {"generation": generation, "member": member, "vector": fit.vector_digest(z), **record,
            "seconds": time.perf_counter() - began}


def _readout_job(item):
    kind, z, seeds = item
    if kind == "wild":
        return kind, fit.simulate_point(z, seeds, assays=None, mutants=(), presence=True)
    return fit._readout_job(item)


def readout(pool, z, seeds, truth):
    """``c2_fit.readout``, with the wild type graded with presence."""
    bands = bg.load_bands_v4()
    parts = dict(pool.map(_readout_job, [(k, z, seeds) for k in ("wild", *fit.MUTANTS)]))
    wild = parts["wild"]
    wild_small = {s: wild["statistics"].get(f"small_lawn/{s}") for s in fit.LAWN_DIFFERENCES}
    wild["delta"] = {
        m: {s: fit._difference(parts[m].get(s), wild_small.get(s)) for s in fit.LAWN_DIFFERENCES}
        for m in fit.MUTANTS
    }
    record = fit.grade_record(wild, bands)
    record["D_truth"] = fit.recovery_distance(truth, record, bands)
    return nr._finite(record)


def _save(path, state):
    tmp = path.with_suffix(".tmp")
    tmp.write_bytes(pickle.dumps(state))
    os.replace(tmp, path)


def _stored(path):
    out = {}
    if path.exists():
        for line in path.read_text().splitlines():
            try:
                r = json.loads(line)
            except json.JSONDecodeError:  # a torn last line from an interrupted write
                continue
            out[r["generation"], r["member"]] = r
    return out


def seeds_of(g, m):
    first = BASE + ANIMALS * (POPSIZE * (g - 1) + m)
    return list(range(first, first + ANIMALS))


def reading(readouts):
    wild = list(readouts[READING[0]]["z"])
    zs = {k: [readouts[g]["z"].get(k) for g in READING] for k in wild}
    undefined = sorted(k for k, v in zs.items() if any(z is None for z in v))
    means = {k: float(np.mean(v)) for k, v in zs.items() if k not in undefined}
    onset = means.get(ONSET)
    others = sorted(k for k, z in means.items() if k != ONSET and abs(z) > WILD_BAR)
    chem = sum(bool((readouts[g].get("chemotaxis_presence") or {}).get("present")) for g in READING)
    search = sum(bool((readouts[g].get("local_search_presence") or {}).get("present")) for g in READING)
    mutants = {m: sum(bool(readouts[g]["mutants"][m]["passes"]) for g in READING) for m in fit.MUTANTS}
    passes = (chem >= 2 and onset is not None and onset >= -WILD_BAR and not others and not undefined
              and search >= 2 and all(n >= 2 for n in mutants.values()))
    return {"generations": list(READING), "chemotaxis_present_of_3": chem, "onset_mean_z": onset,
            "other_failing": others, "undefined": undefined, "local_search_present_of_3": search,
            "mutant_passes_of_3": mutants, "passes": bool(passes)}


def main(workers):
    OUT.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
    state_path, store = OUT / "state.pkl", OUT / "candidates.jsonl"
    bands = bg.load_bands_v4()
    truth = fit.band_truth(bands)
    pilot = json.loads(PILOT.read_text())
    hinge_record = json.loads(HINGE_RECORD.read_text())
    sd = presence_scales(pilot)
    objective = PresenceObjective(truth, bands, families=None, mutants=fit.MUTANTS,
                                  sd=pilot["noise"]["sd"], presence_sd=sd)
    if state_path.exists():
        state = pickle.loads(state_path.read_bytes())
    else:
        hinge_state = pickle.loads(HINGE_STATE.read_bytes())
        previous = hinge_state["es"]
        x0 = [float(v) for v in hinge_record["answer"]]
        assert np.allclose(np.clip(previous.result.xfavorite, 0, 1), x0)
        assert len(hinge_state["medians"]) == 50 and len(x0) == len(fit.entries()) == 137
        state = {"es": fit.carried(previous, x0, CMA_SEED, POPSIZE), "sigma0": float(previous.sigma),
                 "presence_sd": sd, "medians": [], "best": [], "hinge_best": [], "presence_best": [],
                 "sigmas": [], "readouts": {}, "seconds": 0.0}
        _save(state_path, state)
    es = state["es"]

    def read(g):
        z = list(map(float, np.clip(es.result.xfavorite, 0, 1)))
        r = state["readouts"][g] = readout(pool, z, READOUT_SEEDS, truth)
        fails = {k.split("/")[1]: round(v, 2) for k, v in r["z"].items() if v is None or abs(v) > WILD_BAR}
        mp = {m: r["mutants"][m]["passes"] for m in fit.MUTANTS}
        cp = r.get("chemotaxis_presence") or {}
        lb = {k: round(v, 4) for k, v in (cp.get("lower_bounds") or {}).items()}
        print(f"presence screen readout {g}: D_fit {r['D_fit']:.2f} D {r['D']:.2f} "
              f"onset {r['z'].get(ONSET):+.2f} ratio {r['statistics'].get('chemotaxis/log_quintile_ratio'):.3f} "
              f"lower bounds {lb} present {cp.get('present')} "
              f"search {(r.get('local_search_presence') or {}).get('present')} fails {fails} mutants {mp}",
              flush=True)

    with ProcessPoolExecutor(max_workers=workers, initializer=nr._worker_setup) as pool:
        if 0 not in state["readouts"]:
            read(0)
            hinge50 = hinge_record["readouts"]["50"]["statistics"]
            mine = state["readouts"][0]["statistics"]
            same = all(
                (hinge50[k] is None and mine.get(k) is None)
                or (hinge50[k] is not None and mine.get(k) is not None
                    and abs(hinge50[k] - mine[k]) <= 1e-9 * max(1.0, abs(hinge50[k])))
                for k in hinge50
            )
            state["reproduces_hinge_50"] = bool(same)
            print(f"presence screen: generation-0 readout reproduces the hinge screen's generation 50: {same}",
                  flush=True)
            _save(state_path, state)
        while len(state["medians"]) < GENERATIONS:
            g = len(state["medians"]) + 1
            began = time.time()
            points = es.ask()
            candidates = [list(map(float, np.clip(p, 0, 1))) for p in points]
            stored = _stored(store)
            records, todo = {}, []
            for m, z in enumerate(candidates):
                r = stored.get((g, m))
                if r is None:
                    todo.append((g, m, seeds_of(g, m), z))
                elif r["vector"] != fit.vector_digest(z):
                    raise ValueError(f"{store}: generation {g} member {m} is stored at another vector")
                else:
                    records[m] = r
            if todo:
                with store.open("a") as handle:
                    for r in pool.map(_job, todo):
                        handle.write(json.dumps(nr._finite(r), allow_nan=False) + "\n")
                        handle.flush()
                        records[r["member"]] = r
            records = [records[m] for m in range(len(candidates))]
            scores = [objective(r) for r in records]
            es.tell(points, scores)
            best = int(np.argmin(scores))
            state["medians"].append(float(np.median(scores)))
            state["best"].append(float(scores[best]))
            state["hinge_best"].append(float(objective.hinge(records[best])))
            state["presence_best"].append(float(objective.presence(records[best])))
            state["sigmas"].append(float(es.sigma))
            if g % EVERY == 0:
                read(g)
            state["seconds"] += time.time() - began
            print(f"presence screen gen {g}: median {state['medians'][-1]:.2f} best {state['best'][-1]:.2f} "
                  f"(hinge {state['hinge_best'][-1]:.2f}, presence {state['presence_best'][-1]:.2f}) "
                  f"sigma {es.sigma:.4f} {time.time() - began:.0f} s", flush=True)
            _save(state_path, state)
        result = reading(state["readouts"])
        answer = list(map(float, np.clip(es.result.xfavorite, 0, 1)))
        if "grade" not in state:
            jobs = [(answer, list(range(GRADE_BASE + ANIMALS * p, GRADE_BASE + ANIMALS * (p + 1))), True)
                    for p in range(5)]
            state["grade"] = [nr._finite({"population": p, **fit.grade_record(r, bands)})
                              for p, r in enumerate(pool.map(fit._point_job, jobs))]
            _save(state_path, state)
    grade = state["grade"]
    described = {"wild_type": sum(bool(r["wild_type_passes"]) for r in grade),
                 "mutants": {m: sum(bool(r["mutants"][m]["passes"]) for r in grade) for m in fit.MUTANTS},
                 "chemotaxis_present": sum(bool(r["chemotaxis_presence"]["present"]) for r in grade),
                 "ratio_lower_bounds": [r["chemotaxis_presence"]["lower_bounds"]["log_quintile_ratio"]
                                        for r in grade],
                 "onset_z": [r["z"].get(ONSET) for r in grade]}
    record = {
        "description": __doc__, "base": BASE, "cma": CMA_SEED, "grade_base": GRADE_BASE,
        "sigma0": state["sigma0"], "generations": GENERATIONS,
        "hinge": {"weight": HINGE_WEIGHT, "margin": MARGIN},
        "presence_term": {"target": PRESENCE_TARGET, "floor": PRESENCE_FLOOR, "sd": state["presence_sd"],
                          "weights": {"hinge": HINGE_WEIGHT, "failure": fit.FAILURE_WEIGHT}},
        "readout_seeds": [READOUT_SEEDS[0], READOUT_SEEDS[-1]],
        "reproduces_hinge_50": state.get("reproduces_hinge_50"),
        "reading": result, "grade_described": described,
        "medians": state["medians"], "best": state["best"], "hinge_best": state["hinge_best"],
        "presence_best": state["presence_best"], "sigmas": state["sigmas"],
        "readouts": {str(g): v for g, v in sorted(state["readouts"].items())},
        "grade": grade, "answer": answer, "seconds": state["seconds"],
    }
    Path("build/c2-fit/presence_screen.json").write_text(json.dumps(nr._finite(record), indent=1) + "\n")
    print(f"presence screen done: {result}; described grade {described}", flush=True)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main(int(sys.argv[1]) if len(sys.argv) > 1 else 16)

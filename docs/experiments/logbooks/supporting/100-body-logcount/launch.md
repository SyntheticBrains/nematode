# G.0-A — the corrected model's body contrast on log-compressed counts: registration and launch

**Registered 2026-10-19, before any panel run.** Change: `add-body-logcount-contrast`. Tracker
`phase9-tracking` G.0-A; roadmap v4.5 § Phase 9 (G.0's entry, and the Go/No-Go STOP condition), D23,
D24 and D25.

## The question

On log-compressed gap counts, the point worm shows no wiring effect
([Logbook 099](../../099-logcount-contrast.md)): +0.003 [−0.009, +0.016] against a minimum of 0.0577.
The phase's STOP condition reads: *the corrected model shows no wiring effect on either cell under
PPO*. Its other cell is 092's body cell. **Through the body, on the corrected model, does the wild type
still learn faster than its gap-only null?**

## The arms

092's thermal body cell, with two keys changed and nothing else:

- **The cell:** target 20, 500 steps an episode, `body_model: kinematic`, `allow_reversal: true`,
  `action_space: body_drive`, `entropy_coef` 0.004, settling depth 4, 6,000 episodes.
- **The changes:** `connectome_source: emmons_2024_hermaphrodite` (D23; 092 ran on Cook 2019) and
  `gap_weight_mode: log_count` (Logbook 097).

The configs, in `configs/scenarios/thermal_foraging/`, are generated by
`generate_body_logcount_configs.py`:

| arm | config (`connectomeppo_small_continuous2d_thermal_klinotaxis_body_logcount_` + …) | seeds |
|---|---|---|
| wild type, learning | `wt_ppo_t20` | 4001–4128 |
| gap-only null, learning | `gaponly_ppo_t20` | 4001–4128 |
| wild type, frozen | `wt_frozen_t20` | 4001–4016 |
| gap-only null, frozen | `gaponly_frozen_t20` | 4001–4016 |

**288 runs.** The seed bands are new and disjoint from every committed band, which the harness checks:
the panel uses 4001–4128, the gate pilot 9895–9898, and the smokes 9899–9900. The maintainer chose 128
seeds, floors on 16 seeds, and no count-weight arm on 2026-10-19.

## The window, the reading and the verdict map

**The window is episodes 1–3,000**, fixed here and never moved. Under the unmodified learner, every
collapse onset observed on this cell came after episode 3,858: 092's eight, re-run in M.1(a), and the
placebo's two, at 3,913 and 5,625. Under the remedies, one onset came at 2,396 (weight clipping), so a
collapse can begin inside the window. Such a run stays in the reading, and the collapse statistic
reports its onset.

**The reading.** `gap(log, body)` is the per-seed difference, wild type − null, in each run's mean
success over episodes 1–3,000, paired by seed over the 128 seeds. It uses
`wp.paired_seed_wilcoxon_bootstrap` with an 80% percentile interval and the two-sided Wilcoxon p as q
(a family of one), and is classified by `mc.classify` at **m = 0.0577**.

That is the minimum 092 read the body at, and the one the point worm used. It was fixed for
`auc_success` over 6,000 episodes, and it carries to this window because 092's lead was nearly the same
over both: +0.115 to 3,000 and +0.126 to 6,000.

**The gates**, all on the window. The plateau is the instrument's final quarter of episodes 1–3,000,
that is episodes 2,251–3,000.

- **Floor:** each learning arm beats its wiring's frozen floor. The paired interval of learning − frozen
  plateau, over the 16 floor seeds, must lie above zero.
- **Saturation:** the two learning arms' mean plateaus over all 128 seeds must not both be at 90% or
  more.

| state | verdict | with the point worm's `no_wiring_effect` |
|---|---|---|
| gates fail | **`unreadable`** | a dated decision |
| `move_wt` | **`placement`** | whether G.1–G.3b move to the body is a dated decision |
| `below`, either direction | **`below_minimum`** | a dated decision, with the direction recorded |
| `no_move` | **`no_wiring_effect`** | **the STOP branch is taken** |
| `move_null` | **`reversed`** | a dated decision |
| `unresolved` | **`unresolved`** | a dated decision, never a re-read under another rule or window |

**Beside, never verdicts:**

- the same gap over episodes 1–6,000, and over 3,001–6,000;
- episodes to a 30% rolling success rate, censored at 6,000;
- each arm's plateau at 6,000 (episodes 4,501–6,000);
- D25's collapse statistic for each learning arm: incidence with a Wilson interval, Kaplan–Meier time
  to collapse, dips, and 092's own rule;
- the D24 audit, below.

If the late window points elsewhere than the verdict, the verdict stands and the disagreement is
reported.

## Sizing and the operating characteristic

The spread is 092's per-seed sd over episodes 1–3,000, read from its archived logs: **0.242**. That is
the count model on Cook, the committed upper value. On log counts the point worm's spread fell by a
third.

At 128 seeds the standard error is about 0.021, and the detectable effect for the two-sided test at 80%
power is 0.059.

The operating characteristic was simulated with the registered instrument: 600 draws of 128 normal
seed gaps at σ = 0.24 per row, classified by `mc.classify` at m.

| true effect | `placement` | `below_minimum` | `no_wiring_effect` | `unresolved` | `reversed` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0 | 0.05 | 0.79 | 0.15 | 0.01 |
| +0.02 | 0.04 | 0.12 | 0.60 | 0.23 | 0 |
| +0.0577 (the minimum) | 0.50 | 0.23 | 0.07 | 0.20 | 0 |
| +0.08 | 0.85 | 0.11 | 0 | 0.04 | 0 |
| +0.115 (092's early lead) | 1.00 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

The body's per-seed gaps are heavier-tailed than normal, since some learners never take off. The
Wilcoxon q and the percentile bootstrap do not assume normality, so the table is a guide. The achieved
spread is reported beside the reading and never used to re-read it.

## D24: what the null does not preserve, before any run

Computed with `body_logcount_contrast.py table` on the panel's 128 draws ([table.json](table.json)).
The wild type's matrix takes no seed; for the null the table gives the median over draws.

| wiring | max G | p99 | pairs > 1 | ALA total | sensor–motor pairs | count–degree ρ | motors at 1 hop | at 2 hops |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| log-count wild type | 1.34 | 0.73 | 5 | 4.47 | 0 | 0.188 | 0 | 28 |
| log-count gap-only | 1.24 | 0.65 | 2 | 1.38 | 4 | −0.001 | 4 | 38 |

As on the point worm, the null creates about four sensor–motor gap pairs where the wild type has
none. It also drops the count–degree correlation to zero and lowers the hubs' totals. This is stated
beside the verdict and never used to re-read it.

## Before launch

- **Smokes**: every config on seeds 9899–9900 at 20 episodes. All 8 runs exited 0, so the body builds
  and steps on Emmons with log counts. These were execution checks only, and were discarded.

- **Gate pilot**: all four arms on seeds 9895–9898 at 6,000 episodes, configured exactly as the panel.
  All 16 runs exited 0.

- **Gate preflight**: `body_logcount_contrast.py preflight` on the pilot, on the window
  ([preflight.json](preflight.json)). It reads **`readable`**, so launch is **true**.

  - On the window: plateaus of 51.6% (wild type) and 41.3% (null), both floors 0.0%. The highest plateau
    is 38 points under the bar.
  - At 6,000 episodes: 61.2% and 80.1%, reported as gate evidence only.

  The pilot's gap is never read: on four seeds it licenses nothing.

- **Cost**: from the pilot's own records of these configs, the median learning run took 87 minutes and
  the median frozen run 30, at 16 workers. That understates the panel, because half the pilot's
  workers came free after 30 minutes. Under full load, 092's runs on this cell took 125 and 36 minutes.
  The panel's 256 learning and 32 frozen runs therefore come to about **24–34 hours**.

- **Readiness review**: `/nematode-review-spec add-body-logcount-contrast`, 2026-10-19. It found no
  blocking issue. Fixed before registration:

  - the plateau on the window is defined as the final-quarter tail of episodes 1–3,000;
  - which seeds each gate reads is stated: floor beating on the 16 floor seeds, saturation over all 128;
  - frozen runs must also be complete to 6,000 episodes;
  - the manifest check is imported, not copied;
  - why the minimum carries to the window is stated;
  - the table is noted as heavy-tailed.

  Correction to the draft: a collapse onset at episode 2,396 under weight clipping is now cited, so the
  window does not claim to precede every collapse.

## Launch

From a worktree at this commit. The flags and configs are arrays, so the commands work pasted into bash
or zsh.

```bash
git -C ../nematode-g0a checkout --detach <this commit>
cd ../nematode-g0a
uv sync --extra cpu --extra torch --extra pixel --extra analysis --extra qpu
P=configs/scenarios/thermal_foraging/connectomeppo_small_continuous2d_thermal_klinotaxis_body_logcount
OUT=(-- --theme headless --track-experiment --no-detailed-export --no-file-log)
uv run python scripts/run_campaign.py --config "${P}_wt_ppo_t20.yml" --config "${P}_gaponly_ppo_t20.yml" \
  --seeds 4001-4128 --runs 6000 --workers 16 --output-dir ../nematode/campaigns/body-logcount "${OUT[@]}"
uv run python scripts/run_campaign.py --config "${P}_wt_frozen_t20.yml" \
  --config "${P}_gaponly_frozen_t20.yml" --seeds 4001-4016 --runs 6000 --workers 16 \
  --output-dir ../nematode/campaigns/body-logcount "${OUT[@]}"

# Then, from the same worktree, so the registered harness reads the runs:
D=../nematode/docs/experiments/logbooks/supporting/100-body-logcount
uv run python scripts/analysis/body_logcount_contrast.py score --logs ../nematode/campaigns/body-logcount/logs \
  --out-dir build/body-logcount --out $D/panel.json --csv $D/per-seed.csv
```

**Failed runs**: a run that exits non-zero is re-run once on the same seed. `score` reads only a whole
panel, every arm on its seeds with all 6,000 episodes, and refuses a log that does not record the
config its name claims.

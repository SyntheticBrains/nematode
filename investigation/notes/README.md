investigation documents can be traced to a source and to the access level the agent reached. They
were written by agents, not reviewed line by line, and are superseded by the curated documents one
level up wherever the two differ. The only edits are mechanical: em dashes replaced, and scratchpad
paths replaced by this folder's path.

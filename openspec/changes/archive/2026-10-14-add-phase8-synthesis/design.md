### Decision D: Two protocol requirements

Each comes from an 8b defect, as S8a's did.

1. **A panel sized from a small pilot sizes from an upper bound on the pilot's spread.**
   - The evidence: five of the six readings sized from a pilot in 8b achieved a larger spread than
     the pilot's:
     - C.1e, 1.09 times;
     - M.10's lead, 1.5 times;
     - M.10's grounding reading, 1.4 times;
     - M.9, 3.6 times;
     - M.10's receptor reading, the one exception, 0.95 times.
   - M.9's point-estimate sizing gave 9 seeds; the panel's spread would have needed about 111.
2. **A positive control that fails its fixed rule is retried only as a new registration.** M.9's
   retry is the precedent:
   - the failed verdict is kept;
   - the retry's changed settings are fixed before it runs;
   - it runs on new seeds;
   - its failure stops the item.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Phase 10 Living Shipment Checklist

The repository SHALL maintain a single living checklist at `openspec/changes/phase10-tracking/tasks.md`
covering Phase 10's blocks (H, E + T, B, N, F.0, C, W and the synthesis) and the SHOULD and MAY items,
with the seed band reserved for every registration recorded in the change's `design.md`. Every Phase
10 milestone PR SHALL update this file as part of its diff. This change SHALL remain unarchived until
the Phase 10 synthesis publishes.

#### Scenario: A milestone PR omits the checklist

- **WHEN** a Phase 10 milestone PR is opened without an update to `tasks.md`
- **THEN** its review SHALL ask for the update before merge

### Requirement: Design Decisions D33–D41 Are Binding

Every Phase 10 change SHALL conform to the roadmap's D33–D41. An amendment SHALL be dated in the
roadmap and in this change's design before the change that needs it registers.

#### Scenario: A change contradicts a D-decision

- **WHEN** a Phase 10 change's design contradicts D33–D41 without a dated amendment
- **THEN** its spec review SHALL refuse it

### Requirement: The Benchmark Is Void Unless Its Controls Behave

No controller SHALL be graded on the benchmark until cohort-held-out real tracks pass it, held-out
real mutant cohorts pass its mutant grading, and every matched and trivial negative fails it.

#### Scenario: A matched negative passes

- **WHEN** the open-loop HMM or the Poisson-timed primitives pass any composite rule
- **THEN** the benchmark SHALL be recorded void, and revised before any controller is graded

### Requirement: A Fitting Method Clears Parameter Recovery First

No controller SHALL be fitted to real data by a method that has not recovered known parameters from
synthetic data generated by the same model, with its recovery and spread reported.

#### Scenario: A fit on real data registers before recovery

- **WHEN** C.1 or C.2 registers a fit on real data before F.0 has read for its method
- **THEN** the registration SHALL be refused

### Requirement: The Wiring Reading Is at Equal Budget and on Graded Distance

The wild type and every null SHALL be fitted with the same encoder, budget, x₀, σ₀ and plateau
criterion, each null on a fresh rewiring draw. The reading SHALL be the held-out graded distance,
whatever any pass gate reads, against a minimum equal to the held-out distance between two real
wild-type cohorts, in both directions.

#### Scenario: An arm stops at the budget without meeting the plateau criterion

- **WHEN** any arm's fit ends at its budget without meeting the registered plateau criterion
- **THEN** that arm's reading SHALL be reported as unconverged, and SHALL NOT be read as a wiring effect

- [x] I.1b **Annealing the perturbation scale** (`add-l4-sigma-annealing`, 2026-09-10): **`fail`, control valid. - [x] I.3 **The unexamined knobs** (`add-l4-instrument-knobs`, 2026-09-11): **the eligibility horizon is the limiting setting, and raising it recovers the rule.** The registered platform was not usable — on disjoint seeds the MLP yardstick under I.1's rule ends *below its own frozen control*, with no learning signal to vary a knob against — and the horizon was unmeasurable on the undelayed control, which resets the trace every trial (`trace_decay` 0.0/0.9/0.99 identical to four decimals). At `0.99` the same `D = 20` cell closes **45.3%**, a 52-point swing from one setting; `0.999` adds little, so the useful range is bounded. The nominal credit ratio — the decay weight on the scored step, not a measured tensor share — tracks it at Spearman +0.989, which is a correlation and not a threshold: four cells fall below the registered bar and their ratios reach 0.051. **This is the first mechanism in the phase that predicts the central puzzle rather than describing it** — the rule's only success is a one-step task and every multi-step task has failed, at 244 to 2400 steps per episode, orders beyond where the pinned decay already falls below the floor.

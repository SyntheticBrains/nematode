## REMOVED Requirements

Thirty-seven of this capability's forty-four requirements are removed. Six original requirements and one reclassified one remain, and they are exactly what the spec's own Purpose statement has always described: the sequential multi-objective forgetting protocol. The thirty-seven below were registered by eighteen Phase 7 milestone changes because this was the nearest plasticity-named capability, not because the protocol they describe is a property of it.

Each requirement's full text survives in the archived change that added it and in git history. Nothing in `packages/`, `scripts/`, `.github/` or `configs/` references any of them by name, so no consumer breaks; the mapping from each removed requirement to its destination is in this change's `design.md`.

**Retired** (8) — each presupposes a learning rule that writes the substrate through injected per-unit perturbation. That programme closed with a diagnosed cause ([Logbook 063](../../../../../docs/experiments/logbooks/063-l4-eprop.md)): no rule in the family learns this substrate to any benefit, e-prop reaches competence only with the chemical matrix frozen, and Phase 8 does not re-run it. These rules bound work nobody will run again, and a rung designer reading them would be reading about a closed programme.

### Requirement: An annealed perturbation clears the control before the assay

**Reason**: Specific to σ-annealing under node perturbation, screened and failed (Logbook 059's I.1b).
**Migration**: None. The general lesson — a variant clears its positive control before the assay — is principle 4.

### Requirement: A scheduled arm's frozen control runs the same schedule

**Reason**: Specific to scheduled-perturbation arms, which no longer exist.
**Migration**: None. The general lesson — a control matches the arm it bounds — is principle 6.

### Requirement: A perturbing rule's endpoint is evaluated with the perturbation off

**Reason**: Specific to rules that perturb their own units during training.
**Migration**: None. Recorded in Logbook 062 and its archived change.

### Requirement: A perturbing arm is compared with a control at its own setting

**Reason**: As above — a matched-control rule for perturbing arms specifically.
**Migration**: None. The general lesson is principle 6.

### Requirement: A stochastic-gradient result records the perturbation dimension it was measured at

**Reason**: Perturbation-dimension bookkeeping for a rule family that is closed.
**Migration**: None. Recorded in Logbook 060 and its archived change.

### Requirement: A perturbation that cannot reach the scored outcome is not counted as exploration

**Reason**: As above — specific to per-unit perturbation through a forward pass of finite depth.
**Migration**: None. Recorded in Logbook 061 and its archived change.

### Requirement: An eligibility derived from a substrate's own dynamics states what its approximation drops

**Reason**: Specific to e-prop's eligibility, the arm that closed the programme.
**Migration**: None. Recorded in Logbook 063 and its archived change.

### Requirement: A rule whose update needs a per-unit signal registers how that signal reaches each unit

**Reason**: Specific to rules whose eligibility is unsigned with respect to the outcome.
**Migration**: None. Recorded in Logbook 063 and its archived change.

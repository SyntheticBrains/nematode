# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/): while the project is at 0.x, a minor release may contain breaking changes, and they are listed first under each release.

Releases before 0.5.0 are documented on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/SyntheticBrains/nematode/releases); this file starts at 0.5.0.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Decorrelating terms for the three-factor rule, selected by `plasticity_decorrelation` and off by default (`none` is bit-identical). The sign-grounding test found that grounding the substrate's synapse signs made reward-free Hebbian learning substantially worse and predicted that an anti-Hebbian or decorrelating term would recover the loss; this builds the rule and registers the test. The mechanism is now precise: the eligibility trace is sign-carrying, so under random signs an inhibitory synapse driven positive pushed its target negative and was driven further negative in turn — a self-limiting loop that ran on half the substrate — while grounded at 80% excitatory the dominant loop is positive feedback. `anti_hebbian_inhibitory` restores the missing brake using the identity the atlas supplies, negating the Hebbian term at grounded inhibitory synapses only; its magnitude is unchanged, so it redirects the update rather than resizing it, and it has no hyperparameter, so it cannot be tuned into a result. It is refused without grounded signs — by a config validator, by a brain-construction guard because `model_copy` skips validators, and outright on the dense MLP yardstick, which has no identities to key on. `oja` subtracts the classic `η · γ · y² · w` normalisation and needs no identity, reading post-synaptic activity through a new `plastic_post_activities` member on the `PlasticTopology` seam that both substrates expose as a view over state their trace updates already keep. Grounded signs now reach the rule whenever they exist rather than only when Dale's law is enforced, since the two are independent consumers of the same identities. `scripts/analysis/l4_decorrelation.py` fixes the four-arm registry, the D1–D4 BH-FDR family and an ordered verdict map whose first substantive branch is `no_recovery`, so the prediction can fail on the record, with `full_recovery` and the decorrelation share as annotations that cannot change the verdict.

- Consolidation mechanisms for the three-factor rule, one selected at a time by `plasticity_consolidation` and all off by default (`none` is bit-identical and allocates nothing). The minimal rule was shown to be a near-constant-speed drift that passes through good policies and never stops, and its first proposed brake — an update magnitude monotone in the prediction error — was dropped on the committed telemetry: per-step errors are the same size for a competent clone and a mostly-dead random start (median 0.266 against 0.245), so the error carries no information about policy quality, and neither the reward baseline nor episode return does better. The two shippable mechanisms therefore need no quality signal. `anchor` holds a slow moving average of each plastic tensor's own weights and adds a restoring term toward it, which the homeostatic rescale cannot undo as it undoes decay, since the term is direction-dependent rather than a uniform per-unit shrink. `rigidity` grows a per-synapse protective variable where a positive modulator met a large trace — on the trace as the update sees it, so a pinned growth rate is substrate-invariant — and divides the Hebbian rate by `1 + strength · c` at its pre-growth value. `oracle` gates the rate on a trailing episode-success rate reaching the environment's own flag through `post_process_episode`, and is documented throughout as a diagnostic bound rather than a mechanism a nervous system could host. Consolidation state follows a loaded policy — the anchor re-anchors, the protective variable clears — and is never persisted with weights. Three telemetry series report the effective rate multiplier, the anchor departure and the protective variable, so a run that consolidated is distinguishable from one that stopped writing. Three clone-arm configs and `scripts/analysis/l4_consolidation_screen.py` register the clone assay that screens each mechanism: seeds 1–8, 2000 episodes, against the warm-start panel's committed frozen-clone values, reported as a screen that licenses a panel and assigns no verdict. The screen's results are under `docs/experiments/logbooks/supporting/045-l4-consolidation/`: **none of the three passes**. Rigidity comes closest and fails on one clause — 29.4 against the frozen clone's 38.7, six of eight seeds inside the per-seed band, endpoint cosines of 0.67–0.79 against the 0.2–0.45 the unbraked rules produced, and a uniform rate multiplier of 0.09, so a 91% per-synapse rate cut applied exactly where reward had been writing still does not stop the drift. The anchor does not hold the policy at all (13.0, none of eight at or above). The oracle failed in the way its launch record predicted before the run: a trailing estimate at an EMA rate of 0.01 arrives a hundred episodes after a policy this rule destroys in tens, and where the gate did close it worked, so a fast quality-gated brake is untested rather than refuted. Loading also stopped refusing weight files written before sign grounding existed — a missing sign buffer is now read as the all-zero buffer it can only be, while a cross-sign-model load is still refused — without which the assay could not load the clones it starts from.

- Neurotransmitter identities on the connectome substrate, the first rung of the fidelity ladder. The Wang et al. 2024 CRISPR knock-in atlas is vendored under `data/connectome/` with full provenance, from the same MIT-licensed OpenWorm mirror as Cook 2019; a reader normalises its editorial annotation, maps its two joint motor-neuron labels and treats uptake as distinct from release; a generation script writes the identities into the committed 302-entry classification table (whose transmitter slot had been unpopulated since Phase 6) and a test re-derives them from the file, so nothing at run time reads the spreadsheet. `synapse_signs: atlas` on the connectome brain replaces each chemical weight's drawn sign with the one its pre-synaptic transmitter implies — 3,176 of 3,709 synapses grounded, 2,962 excitatory and 214 inhibitory, taking the network from 48% inhibitory to 13% while magnitudes, per-neuron incoming norms, the readout, the gains and the RNG stream stay identical. `enforce_synapse_signs` adds Dale's law during plasticity, projecting each update onto its synapse's sign before the homeostatic rescale and refused unless the signs are grounded. Six arm configs and `scripts/analysis/l4_atlas_signs.py` register the test, whose results are under `docs/experiments/logbooks/supporting/044-l4-atlas-signs/`: verdict `degree_statistics` — the prior over untrained policies is indifferent to signs (the registered substrate-failure outcome did not occur), the wiring contrast is not rescued, and reward-free Hebbian learning gets substantially worse under real signs (wild-type 31.5 → 14.0), with Dale's law worse again, because a purely potentiating rule on a mostly-excitatory network has no inhibitory brake.

- The warm-start panel's tooling. `weights_path` accepts `{seed}`, resolved with the run seed by the entry point, so one committed config addresses one clone file per seed. Twelve configs, each one key off a parent: the frozen, Hebbian and three-factor arms on both wirings warm-started from the plastic-set clone (`_clone`), the frozen arm on both wirings from the full-set clone (`_fullclone`), the PPO arm on both wirings at the plastic arms' initial noise (`_lowstd`, the from-scratch comparator and the full-set clone's student), and PPO fine-tuning from the full-set clone on both wirings. `scripts/campaigns/l4_warm_start_campaign.py` selects the teacher (the MLP-PPO seed with the highest plateau tail), records it frozen with its action means, and clones every student at fixed hyperparameters with every fit recorded; `scripts/analysis/l4_warm_start.py` fixes the twelve-arm registry, a six-test BH-FDR family (a clone-competence gate, wiring representability, the warm-started wild-type-over-rewired three-factor primary, two sanity floors from the clone, and warm-started PPO against scratch), the ordered verdict map (`clone_fail` and `sanity_floor_fail` ahead of the wiring verdicts) and the `rule_destroys_clone` annotation before any data exist. The panel's results under `docs/experiments/logbooks/supporting/043-l4-warm-start/`: verdict `sanity_floor_fail` with `rule_destroys_clone` — the clone gate passes on every seed (the connectome holds a competent policy: 39% through the chemical weights alone, 74% with every PPO parameter, and the rewired null holds it at least as well), the three-factor rule takes the clone apart on both wirings, the Hebbian rule holds it on the wild-type only, and fine-tuning with PPO from the clone loses to PPO from random weights at the same low noise, which itself reaches 69–81% in 3000 episodes. The load path now re-anchors the plastic rule's homeostatic targets to the loaded weights, a defect the panel's first plastic-set run exposed.

- Warm-start tooling for the imitation arm, every piece byte-identical when unused. `ConnectomePPOBrain` implements `WeightPersistence` (closes #308): a `topology` component with every parameter and the wiring buffers (never the per-episode trace buffers, so a file saved under either rule loads under the other), `value` and `optimizer` only while PPO is live, and a `training_state` record; a load validates the std mode and the wiring before mutating anything, resets the rollout buffer, and under the plastic rule calls the new `ThreeFactorRule.reset_state` (baseline, both running scales, the centring mean, the traces). `ActionData.continuous_mean` reports the noiseless bounded action beside the sample on the MLP-PPO and connectome brains. `--record-rollouts PATH` on the simulation entry point attaches a `RolloutRecorder` that writes one JSON line per step (the `BrainParams` the brain read, the sampled action, the mean, the probability), flushed per episode; the runners make no call without it. `scripts/campaigns/l4_behavioural_clone.py` builds a student from a config at a seed as the entry point does, preprocesses recorded observations through the student's own feature path and batched forward, minimises the action-space mean squared error against the teacher's recorded mean over the `plastic` set (chemical weights only, masked to the wiring) or the `full` set (everything PPO trains except the noise parameter, which a mean-only record cannot target), holds out a seeded fraction of episodes, refuses to save without improvement, and saves through `save_weights` with a clone record beside the file.

- `scripts/analysis/l4_panel3.py`, the third L4 panel's analysis fixed before any data exist: a replication of the reward-free Hebbian wiring contrast on seeds 17–64, fresh for this contrast (panel 2 ran only its frozen prior sweep on them, never the Hebbian arms), with a two-test BH-FDR family — the committed paired Wilcoxon as the primary and an exact binomial on competent-fraction discordance (at panel 2's 20% threshold) as a registered secondary — the verdict assigned from the primary alone in the rewired-null control's vocabulary, the frozen floors and the descriptive pooling of seeds 1–16 read from panel 2's committed per-seed table so the analysis reproduces from the repository, a completeness flag per test, and the single-extension list. The panel's results under `docs/experiments/logbooks/supporting/042-l4-panel3/`: verdict `inconclusive` — +8.1 on seeds 17–64 (interval clear of zero, q = .19, 24 of 48 seeds positive), the estimate shrinking with each fresh look from panel 1's +16.2; pooled over 64 seeds +9.6 with competent fractions 0.39 against 0.25; the wild-type's advantage sits in the level of its good fixed points, not their frequency. No run needed an extension.

- `weight_init` on the connectome brain config: `degree_scaled` (default, byte-identical) or `count_scaled`, which initialises each chemical weight as a standard-normal draw times `n / sqrt(Σ n²)` over the post-synaptic neuron's incoming EM synapse counts — magnitudes proportional to count, signs still random, and the same expected squared incoming norm of 1 as the degree-scaled draw, so homeostatic targets are unchanged and only the structure within a neuron's inputs differs. Chemical weights only; under the degree-preserving rewiring each count travels with its edge. Four `_countinit` configs put the frozen and unmodulated-Hebbian floors on both wirings under it. `scripts/analysis/l4_panel2.py` fixes the second L4 panel's analysis before any data exist: eight arms with per-arm seed ranges (Hebbian 1–16, frozen 1–64), a four-test BH-FDR family with the wild-type-over-rewired Hebbian contrast as the primary, a verdict assigned from that test alone, the prior sweep's per-arm distributions and competent fractions, learning gains against each arm's own floor, and the list of runs owed the single registered extension. The panel's results, as computed by that harness, under `docs/experiments/logbooks/supporting/041-l4-panel2/` (`panel2.json`, `per-seed.csv`, `curves.csv`, the manifest, the launch record, `details.md`): verdict `inconclusive` — the wild-type Hebbian arm beats its rewired null by +14.1 points on 10 of 16 seeds with an interval clear of zero, but a bimodal outcome leaves the test at q = .277; the 64-seed prior sweep puts the untrained wild-type only +3.3 above its null; and count-scaled initialisation reverses on the wild-type Hebbian arm (−15.6) while leaving every frozen prior unchanged.

- The L4 2×2 panel's results, as computed by the registered harness, under `docs/experiments/logbooks/supporting/040-l4-panel/` (`panel.json`, `per-seed.csv`, `curves.csv`, the manifest, the launch record, the pilot and probe records, `details.md`): seven arms × eight paired seeds at 3000 episodes, verdict `sanity_floor_fail` — the plastic wild-type arm does not beat its unmodulated-Hebbian floor at n = 8, which itself reaches 64–78% on three seeds with no reward. The harness gains a descriptive per-seed `peak_action_density` column read from each run's export, so a collapsed representation (actions pinned at the squash limits) is visible per arm.

- `plastic_layers` on the MLP brain config: `all` (default, byte-identical) or `hidden`, which keeps the output layer off the plastic-topology seam — no trace, mask, fan-in axis, homeostatic target or update — so the matched-rule yardstick learns its hidden weights behind a fixed decoder as the connectome does. Motivated by a foraging-only probe in which a frozen tanh MLP foraged at 96% from its initialisation while the same network with a plastic output layer collapsed to zero within three episodes, its actions pinned at the squash limits: a plastic output layer takes its own output as its post-synaptic factor and rotates into saturation under the rule. Reverses the all-layers choice made when the arm was added.

- Three mechanisms that keep the plastic arms honest before the L4 panel, each default-off or default-unchanged and byte-identical. `initial_log_std` on the shared plasticity mixin sets the action noise the state-independent Gaussian head starts at on both plastic brains (the plastic arms and floors were exploring at std 1.0 for their whole run); it is ignored in discrete mode and a non-zero value is rejected under the state-dependent std head. `plasticity_homeostasis` rescales each unit's incoming plastic weights, over its edge set only, to the norm they had when the rule was built after every update — synaptic scaling, which stops Hebbian positive feedback running a fifth of the synapses onto the bound and exactly undoes the decay term — through a new `plastic_fan_in_axes` seam property (connectome 0, MLP 1) and reported as `plasticity_norm_drift`. `activation` on the MLP brain config builds `tanh` hidden units (gain 5/3) so the matched-rule yardstick has bounded units like the connectome's instead of ReLU units that explode or die under a local rule.

- The compressed modulator of the three-factor rule is now centred: under `plasticity_normalise_modulator` the third factor is `tanh(δ/σ) − c`, with c a bias-corrected running mean of the compressed value from a zero prior, used at its pre-update value. A bounded function of a skewed prediction error is not zero-mean (a −10 death and a +2 food both compress to ±1, and foods are more frequent), so the uncentred form carried a steady reward-blind Hebbian drive that collapsed the connectome and blew up the MLP in the first probe with the switches on; centring restores the zero mean a prediction error must have. One telemetry key, `plasticity_modulator_centre`; no new switch; the raw rule stays byte-identical.

- Substrate-invariant scaling for the three-factor rule, two opt-in switches on the shared plasticity mixin. `plasticity_normalise_modulator` makes the third factor `tanh(δ/σ)`, σ a bias-corrected running RMS of the raw prediction error: bounded, sign-preserving, and no longer dominated by the one terminal penalty that is two hundred times an ordinary step. `plasticity_normalise_trace` divides each plastic tensor's Hebbian term by ρ, a bias-corrected running RMS of its eligibility trace over its edge set, so the plasticity rate means the same root-mean-square step per unit modulator on the connectome and on the MLP, whose traces differ by three orders of magnitude. Both scales are scored against their pre-update value, advance under a freeze and in unmodulated mode, and skip all-zero traces; three new telemetry keys report the effective modulator and the two scales. Both switches off is byte-identical to the raw rule, proven against the frozen reference. Motivated by the L4 panel's pilot, where the raw rule clamped the Hebbian floor at 96% saturation and killed the MLP yardstick in its first episode.

- The L4 panel tooling. Two configs complete the wiring × rule factorial — the frozen-weights and unmodulated-Hebbian floors on degree-preserving rewired-null wiring, each one `wiring` key off its wild-type floor and, at one seed, on exactly the mask, gap matrix and initial weights the plastic rewired-null arm learns on. `scripts/analysis/l4_panel.py` fixes the panel's confirmatory analysis in code before any panel data exist: a four-test BH-FDR family (plastic wild-type over plastic rewired-null; over its frozen and Hebbian floors; wild-type learning gain over rewired learning gain), a CI-based matched-rule-MLP band test reported with its mean and interval width, an ordered verdict map, per-seed sign counts, and every other pair labelled descriptive; confirmatory mode refuses seeds outside 1–8. Its pilot mode applies the pre-registered recipe-selection and budget rules. `scripts/campaigns/l4_panel_pilot.py` derives the rate-grid configs beside the results and drives `run_campaign.py` with experiment tracking on.

- The plastic degree-preserving rewired-null arm (`connectomeppo` with `learning_rule: three_factor` and `wiring: rewired_degree_preserving`): the other cell of the Phase 7 panel's primary contrast, one key different from the plastic wild-type arm. No new mechanism — the rewiring precedes topology construction, so the mask, weight support, eligibility traces and every plastic update sit on the null wiring by construction. Specified and tested at the brain level on the real C3 configs: at one seed the two plastic arms share the anatomical readout, every sensory-projection gain, `log_std`, trace configuration and hyperparameters, and preserve every in/out and gap degree; they differ in the mask, initial weight placement and gap-junction matrix. Per-neuron initial weight *energy* is deliberately not claimed preserved — it is not. Pairing is by run seed.

- The matched-rule MLP arm (`mlpppo` with `learning_rule: three_factor`): the same reward-modulated three-factor rule, now substrate-generic over a `PlasticTopology` seam (aligned plastic weights, eligibility traces, and edge masks) instead of naming the connectome's attributes. An `MLPTopology` wraps the actor's Linear layers by reference — same object, no new state-dict keys, no re-run of construction — so the PPO path is byte-identical to a pre-refactor frozen reference. Feedforward eligibility is the same-step output-by-input product per layer; every Linear weight is plastic, biases and `log_std` frozen, critic dormant. The plasticity hyperparameters (and `freeze_updates`) move to a mixin both brain configs inherit, so "matched" means the same defaults from one definition. `freeze_updates` is now honoured on the MLP under PPO as well, so the flag means one thing on every brain.

- Two sanity-floor arms for the connectome plasticity panel: a **frozen-weights** floor (`freeze_updates` under the plasticity rule — no learning term, decay or clamp, so weights stay bit-identical to initialisation) and an **unmodulated-Hebbian** floor (`learning_rule: hebbian` — `dw = eta * E`, the reward stream observed but never applied). Both are configured under the plasticity rule rather than PPO so they share its anatomical motor readout: a floor decoding differently from the arm it bounds would confound decoding with learning. The unmodulated arm still computes and reports its prediction error, so the ablation is visible in telemetry rather than only in configuration. Together they separate "this arm learned something" from "this arm learned something from reward".

- A reward-modulated three-factor learning rule for the connectome brain (`learning_rule: three_factor`, requiring `enable_activity_traces`): `dw = eta * delta * E`, where `E` is the topology's eligibility trace and `delta` is a reward prediction error against a running baseline. It computes no gradients, owns no optimiser or value head, and updates once per environment step. Only the chemical synapses change — sensory gains and the motor readout stay frozen, the latter at the anatomical dorsal/ventral and forward/backward contrasts of the motor pools rather than a random draw, since a decoder that is never trained should respect what those pools mean. Plasticity is bounded by weight decay and a magnitude clamp, with prediction error, baseline, mean weight change and saturated fraction reported per update. Dale's law is deliberately not imposed: initial synapse signs here are arbitrary draws rather than neurotransmitter identity, so constraining them would preserve noise. Ships with one plastic wild-type config; the comparison arms follow.

- `scripts/run_campaign.py`: runs a campaign of simulations — one or more configs crossed with a set of seeds — concurrently under a bounded worker pool, with per-run logs, progress reporting, a status summary, `--dry-run`, and a non-zero exit if any run fails. Each run is a subprocess of `run_simulation.py` given byte-for-byte the command line it replaces, so campaigns change only *when* runs happen, never what they compute. Measured 7.29x at 16 workers on an 18-core machine ([Logbook 039](docs/experiments/logbooks/039-runtime-acceleration-audit.md)).

- `DeviceType.MPS` (`--device mps`): Apple's Metal GPU is now selectable for PyTorch brains, and therefore measurable. CPU remains the default and is faster for every brain currently in the repo — see the device benchmark below.

- `scripts/benchmarks/bench_device_backends.py` and `scripts/benchmarks/bench_campaign_parallelism.py`: reproducible measurements behind the device and worker-count guidance, including a large-network control row that distinguishes an unsuitable workload from a broken accelerator.

- The state-dependent continuous action std (roadmap D7): a `continuous_std_mode` field on every brain config (default `state_independent`, byte-identical to previous behaviour; the discrete combination fails validation), per-brain zero-init std heads on the five continuous brains, six `_sdstd` config variants, and a per-update clamped-log-std monitor in the tracked telemetry. Ships **dormant**: the pre-registered klinokinesis validation gate failed (Logbook 038), so the Phase 7 panel substrate froze in the default mode and the mechanism awaits a future pre-registered retry.

- The Phase 7 L4 trace substrate on the connectome brain: `enable_activity_traces` and `trace_decay` config fields (off by default; per-synapse eligibility traces accumulated during rollout forwards for the upcoming three-factor rules — training is bit-identical with traces on until a rule consumes them), and the new `quantumnematode.learning_rules` package, whose first citizen `ConnectomePPORule` is the connectome PPO update extracted byte-identically behind the `LearningRule` seam.

### Changed

- Roadmap decision D2 described the frozen-weights baseline as using "Cook-2019 synapse-count-derived initial weights". The implementation has never done that: the connectome supplies which edges exist, weights along them are drawn `N(0, 1/sqrt(chemical in-degree))`, and the EM synapse count never reaches the weight matrix. The wording is corrected rather than the initialisation — changing the latter would move a substrate that existing results are recorded against.

- Connectome configs now appear in the smoke-test set. None did previously, so the plasticity code path — per-step updates, no value head, an unused rollout buffer — had never been exercised end to end through the run entry point.

- The connectome's eligibility trace is now temporally causal: `E <- lambda*E + M(h_prev (x) h)`, taking the previous step's settled state as the pre-synaptic factor instead of a same-step symmetric outer product. The adjacency mask already separated non-reciprocal edges, but the symmetric form gave both directions of a reciprocal pair identical eligibility and encoded no temporal order, which a rule crediting synapses for causing activity cannot rest on. The first step of an episode now accrues no eligibility. Traces remain off by default and, while no rule consumes them, training remains bit-identical to traces-off.

- Device selection is validated before brain construction and fails with an actionable message. Previously `--device gpu` mapped unconditionally to CUDA and crashed on non-CUDA hosts with a raw `AssertionError: Torch not compiled with CUDA enabled`, despite being an advertised CLI choice. Selection is also checked against the brain family: a PyTorch-only accelerator is rejected for quantum brains, which pass the device to Qiskit — `AerSimulator(device="MPS")` is accepted *without raising*, so the bogus backend would otherwise have been recorded in experiment metadata as though it were real.

- Connectome-brain runs now record the mean policy loss per PPO update in the tracked `losses` telemetry (the house convention; the brain previously recorded no loss), so connectome session exports gain a `losses` column.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-08-23

The pre-Phase-7 housekeeping release. It takes the platform that closed Phase 6a, removes the research tooling the platform no longer needs, moves the toolchain to Python 3.13 and Qiskit 2, and prepares the repository for a wider audience.

### Breaking changes

- **NematodeBench removed** ([#274](https://github.com/SyntheticBrains/nematode/pull/274)). The curated benchmark submission workflow, validation, category and leaderboard generation, `BENCHMARKS.md` and `docs/nematodebench/` are gone: across Phases 5 and 6 the architecture-comparison protocol read `--track-experiment` output directly and never used them. The one live component, the convergence detector and composite score, moved to `quantumnematode.experiment.convergence`; the 72 tracked sessions behind the old submissions were migrated to `artifacts/experiments/`; the `composite_benchmark_score` key is unchanged so historical artifacts still load.
- **`qqlearning` brain retired** ([#286](https://github.com/SyntheticBrains/nematode/pull/286)). `QQLearningBrain` was the last architecture with no path to the Phase 7 plasticity work. Configs with `brain.name: qqlearning` no longer load (the loader rejects unregistered brain types); 26 architectures remain, and every count in the docs is now derived from the registry. No tracked artifact carries the retired name.
- **Python 3.13 only, and the `gpu` extra moves to CUDA 11** ([#289](https://github.com/SyntheticBrains/nematode/pull/289)). `requires-python` is `>=3.13,<3.14` (it was pinned to 3.12). The CUDA-12 `qiskit-aer-gpu` build was abandoned upstream after 0.15.1 and has no 3.13 wheel, so the `gpu` extra now installs `qiskit-aer-gpu-cu11` (0.17.2): the bundled CUDA runtime goes from 12 to 11, which needs an NVIDIA driver ≥ 450 rather than ≥ 525. Every dependency was refreshed at the same time (torch 2.13, numpy 2.5, pydantic 2.13, scipy 1.18, scikit-learn 1.9, matplotlib 3.11, rich 15, optuna 4.9).
- **Qiskit 2.x** ([#290](https://github.com/SyntheticBrains/nematode/pull/290)). `qiskit>=2.0,<3`. The platform needed no code changes (it never used the APIs Qiskit 2 removed), but anything of yours built on Qiskit 1.x removals — `BackendV1`, `execute()`, `bind_parameters()`, `opflow`, `qiskit.pulse` — must migrate. Test-suite deprecation warnings dropped from ~382,000 to ~200.

### Security

- `SECURITY.md` documents private vulnerability reporting and what is unsafe to load from untrusted sources ([#296](https://github.com/SyntheticBrains/nematode/pull/296)).
